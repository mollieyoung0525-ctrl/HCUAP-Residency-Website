## History of HCUAP

10 years ago, Dr. Caitlin Bruce of the University of Pittsburgh, a scholar of public art, realized that graffiti, street art, and murals could start conversations. After traveling in Latin America and Chicago and doing extensive interviews with graffiti writers and muralists, Bruce saw that these conversations weren’t happening in Pittsburgh–a city with one of the harshest graffiti policies in the country. 

Working with Shane Pilster and Oreen Cohen, she launched HCUAP (Hemispheric Conversations Urban Arts Project). Now, 10 years later, HCUAP is still going strong.

HCUAP, pronounced "hiccup," seeks to create platforms for conversation and education about urban art production (graffiti, street art, and muralism, among other genres), and to explore aesthetic and historical connections between post-industrial cities around the world. HCUAP is one of the world’s only mural and graffiti  residencies. With muralist Max Gonzaleas and art writer Emma Riva as new collaborators over the past five years, the residency has now grown to feature artists from Spain, the Philippines, Argentina, and Mexico. 

## 2026 Visiting Artist Residency 

This year’s iteration featured Ish Muhammad (Gary, IN), Gloria Talamantes (Chicago, IL), and Tomás Ives (Santiago, Chile). The artists were in Pittsburgh from 9/17 to 9/28, with a packed events calendar along the way. The 2026 residency is titled Our Americas, in the spirit of interrogating America 250 and the understanding of borders between North and South America. 

Muhammad, Ives, and Talamentes produced murals around the city, including on Strawberry Way downtown in collaboration with Pittsburgh Downtown Partnership.
