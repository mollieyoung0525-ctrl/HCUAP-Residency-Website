## Preserving HCUAPs Residencies Legacy

Each year, the Hemispheric Conversations: Urban Art Project (HCUAP), brings artists from around the world together in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, for a week long residency aimed at promoting conversations about and the creation of urban art. Residents participate in a number of workshops, tours, and events, and ultimately each complete a mural based on their experiences. The murals left behind by the residency are now part of Pittsburgh's visual landscape, this project aims to digitize them and preserve the legacy of the residency that extends beyond the images themselves, into the relationships formed, the conversations opened and the people invited to imagine what their shared spaces might become.
